SUPERCAMPUS AUTHORITY AND PORTAL DEVELOPMENT PLAN
=================================================

Decision
--------
A portal is a stable experience for a category of user. A role is a configurable
authority bundle inside that portal. Do not create a separate frontend application
for every college job title.

The canonical portal families are:

1. Student Portal
   Personal academics and campus services. Scope is always own data.

2. Parent Portal
   Linked-student summaries, notices, payments and approvals. Scope is limited to
   explicitly linked students.

3. Staff Operations Portal
   One permission-driven workspace shared by Principal, HOD, Accountant,
   Admissions Officer, Faculty, Verifier, Librarian, Warden and similar roles.
   Navigation, dashboards, actions and data are derived from effective permissions.

4. Tenant Admin Portal
   College-level configuration for users, roles, branding, forms, workflows,
   integrations and audit. Tenant Admin is not a platform operator.

5. Platform Control Portal
   A separate internal SuperCampus application for Platform SuperAdmins only.
   It operates across tenants and must never be assignable by a college administrator.

Authority Model
---------------
Platform SuperAdmin  -> Platform Control Portal -> cross-tenant scope
Tenant Admin         -> Tenant Admin Portal      -> one institution
Principal            -> Staff Operations Portal -> institution scope
Management           -> Staff Operations Portal -> sensitive management approvals
Academic Administrator -> Staff Operations Portal -> institution academic configuration
HOD                  -> Staff Operations Portal -> department scope
Accountant           -> Staff Operations Portal -> finance permissions, institution scope
Admissions Officer   -> Staff Operations Portal -> assigned records or admissions team
Faculty              -> Staff Operations Portal -> assigned classes and subjects
Student              -> Student Portal          -> own data
Parent/Guardian      -> Parent Portal           -> linked students only

Confirmed Governance Decisions (17 August 2026)
------------------------------------------------
- Principal approves fee decisions (excluding refunds), result publication and
  student suspension. Principal does not approve admissions.
- Accountant prepares finance operations and refund requests but cannot approve
  or issue refunds. Final refund approval belongs only to Management.
- HOD sees the assigned department plus cross-department subjects taught by staff
  belonging to that department.
- Principal or Academic Administrator assigns departments to HODs and classes or
  subjects to Faculty. Principal is the usual operational owner.
- Sensitive authority checks require both an explicit permission and the allowed
  governance role. Tenant configuration cannot bypass separation of duties.

The Principal, HOD and Accountant may receive different landing dashboards, but
they use the same Staff Operations application and component library. A dashboard
is a role-aware arrangement of permitted widgets, not a separate portal.

Access Evaluation
-----------------
Effective access is evaluated in this order:

1. Account must be active and belong to the tenant.
2. Portal family must match the route being opened.
3. Role grants provide module, feature and action permissions.
4. Direct user grants may add or deny exceptional access.
5. Scope limits every query: own, assigned, department, institution or all.
6. Backend authorization is authoritative; hiding UI is not security.
7. Sensitive writes create immutable audit events.

Avoid role-name checks such as "if principal" in domain features. Check explicit
permissions and scope. Role templates are defaults that tenant admins may copy and
adjust without changing portal code.

Development Phases
------------------
PHASE 0 - Preserve and Stabilize
- Keep current CRM, Application Desk, forms, branding and mobile sync contracts.
- Add contract tests before changing shared navigation, authentication or RBAC.
- Remove preview data from live operational paths.
- Standardize loading, empty, denied, timeout and retry states.

PHASE 1 - Authority Foundation
- Publish canonical role templates in Access Control.
- Add department and assigned-record constraints to grants.
- Resolve landing dashboard from portal family and effective permissions.
- Add access-preview tooling: "View portal as this role" without impersonation.
- Add permission-change and role-assignment audit events.

PHASE 2 - Student 360 Master
- Make Student Master the canonical tenant student record.
- Add profile sections: identity, contacts, guardians, academics, documents,
  welfare, accommodation and campus services.
- Add completeness rules and a readiness score based on tenant-required fields.
- Add configurable lifecycle statuses with reasons and full history.
- Link admission onboarding idempotently to exactly one Student Master record.

PHASE 3 - Academic Operations
- Programme, department, batch, section, subject and curriculum configuration.
- Timetable allocation linking student, staff, room and subject.
- Student schedule and staff teaching schedule from the same allocation records.
- Attendance exceptions, leave workflow, marks, results and backlog processing.
- Department-scoped HOD dashboards and assigned-class Faculty dashboards.

PHASE 4 - Finance Operations
- Fee structures, student ledgers, invoices, collections and receipts.
- Payment allocation, bank/payment-mode reconciliation and unadjusted balances.
- Scholarships, concessions, refunds and approval rules.
- Outstanding-fee lists with controlled reminder delivery.
- Accountant dashboard with finance-only permissions and audit trails.

PHASE 5 - Reports and Operational Intelligence
- Permission-gated report catalogue and saved report filters.
- Institution, department and assigned scopes applied by the backend.
- Principal dashboard for institution health and approvals.
- HOD dashboard for department academics, attendance and student risk.
- Accountant dashboard for collections, ageing and reconciliation.
- Drill-down from every metric to the underlying authorised records.

PHASE 6 - Platform Control Portal
- Tenant provisioning, suspension and domain management.
- Subscription and usage controls.
- Platform health, migration status and integration health.
- Support tooling with explicit, time-limited audited tenant access.
- Global security and audit review.
- No college operational data access by default.

Cross-Cutting Capabilities
--------------------------
- Reusable bulk import: template, validate, preview, commit, row errors and audit.
- Reusable document/media service with tenant ownership and malware validation.
- Notifications with in-app, email, SMS and WhatsApp channels.
- Idempotency for imports, payments, onboarding and external callbacks.
- Immutable audit log for deletion, access, finance, results and status changes.
- Export controls and masking for sensitive personal information.

First Deliverable Order
-----------------------
1. Complete permission catalog and scoped query enforcement.
2. Role templates and role-aware dashboard composition.
3. Student 360 data model and read-only profile.
4. Student lifecycle status and completeness engine.
5. Academic structures and timetable allocator.
6. Finance ledger and reconciliation foundation.
7. Reports catalogue and audit explorer.
8. Platform Control Portal.

Current Foundation Status (17 August 2026)
------------------------------------------
Completed:
- Canonical tenant role templates for Tenant Admin, Principal, HOD, Accountant,
  Admissions Officer, Faculty, Student and Parent/Guardian.
- Platform SuperAdmin is explicitly excluded from tenant role creation.
- Role templates prefill portal, team and default scope but never auto-grant
  module or action permissions.
- Effective portal-family routing for student, staff and tenant-admin sessions,
  with a compatibility fallback for legacy identities.
- Full scope contract support for own, assigned, department, institution and all.
- Confirmed governance policy for Principal, Management, Accountant, HOD and
  Academic Administrator, with additive permission catalog definitions and no
  automatic grants.
- Canonical tenant-isolated subjects and section-level subject offerings linked
  to the existing academic year, term, department, programme, batch and section
  records.
- Governed HOD-to-department and Faculty-to-subject-offering assignments with
  removal history in the academic assignment audit log.
- One scoped academic context API: Students resolve offerings from active
  section enrolment, Faculty from teaching assignments, HODs from their assigned
  departments plus cross-department subjects taught by their staff, and
  institution authorities from their explicit institution/all grant.
- Typed admin-web API contract for subject, offering, HOD and Faculty assignment
  operations. Write access requires both the permission and the Principal or
  Academic Administrator governance role.
- Tenant-configured timetable calendars, working days, instructional periods,
  lunch and break slots. The initial Anna University 2025 policy preset uses a
  configurable Faculty daily load of 6 and a configurable consecutive-period
  limit of 3; these are tenant policy values, not hard-coded regulations.
- Tenant room and space inventory with department ownership, room type,
  capacity, features and active status for allocator conflict checks.
- Cross-section elective groups with explicit section and Student membership,
  so a shared elective remains traceable and appears in every affected schedule.
- Draft, published, superseded and archived timetable versions, with scheduled
  entries linked to subject offerings, Faculty assignments, rooms and optional
  elective groups.
- Publication validation for Faculty, section, room, capacity, daily workload
  and consecutive-period conflicts before a version can become authoritative.
- One scoped timetable context API: Students receive their active section and
  elective schedule; Faculty receive assigned and approved-substitution classes;
  HODs receive their department plus cross-department teaching by their Faculty;
  institution authorities receive institution scope only when explicitly granted.
- Digital Faculty substitution requests with original-Faculty and replacement-
  Faculty acknowledgements, Principal-only approval or rejection, and a complete
  immutable event trail corresponding to the existing signed paper process.
- Monotonic timetable revision events and a changes endpoint for live polling;
  web and mobile clients can refresh only when a newer revision is available.
- Typed admin-web API contract for configurations, slots, rooms, elective groups,
  timetable versions, entries, publication, scoped context and substitutions.

Definition of Done for Every Module
-----------------------------------
- Backend permission and scope are enforced.
- Tenant isolation has an automated test.
- Loading, empty, denied and error states are implemented.
- Bulk operations are idempotent where relevant.
- Sensitive actions create audit records.
- Navigation appears only with effective read permission.
- Existing CRM, Application Desk and mobile contracts continue to pass.
- Desktop and mobile layouts are visually verified.
