# Alumni Management Workflow

## Overview

The Alumni Management module connects graduates with the institution through registration, directory services, mentorship programs, donation campaigns, events & reunions, and engagement analytics.

## Individual Workflows

### 1. Alumni Registration Workflow

| Step | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Graduate Student** | Student completes their degree |
| 2 | **Register as Alumni** | Sign up on the alumni portal |
| 3 | **Verify Email / Mobile** | Confirm contact information |
| 4 | **Complete Alumni Profile** | Fill in personal and professional details |
| 5 | **Academic Details** | Batch • Programme • Department |
| 6 | **Professional Details** | Company • Designation • Industry |
| 7 | **Profile Verification** | Admin reviews and validates the profile |
| 8 | **Approved** | Profile is activated |
| 9 | **Added to Alumni Directory** | Graduate becomes part of the alumni network |

### 5. Donations & Contributions Workflow

| Step | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Create Donation Campaign** | Define purpose and target amount |
| 2 | **Notify Alumni** | Send campaign announcements |
| 3 | **Online Contribution** | Alumni make donations through the portal |
| 4 | **Payment Confirmation** | System verifies the transaction |
| 5 | **Generate Receipt** | Auto-generate tax receipt |
| 6 | **Allocate Fund** | Direct funds to the designated cause |
| 7 | **Contribution History** | Maintain records of all donations |

#### Donation Examples

## Key Features Summary

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Alumni Registration** | Self-service portal for graduates to register and verify profiles |
| **Alumni Directory** | Searchable directory with filters (batch, department, company, location) |
| **Alumni Engagement** | Announcements, newsletters, forums, success stories, and awards |
| **Mentorship Program** | Alumni volunteer as mentors matched with students |
| **Donations & Contributions** | Campaign creation, online payments, receipts, and fund allocation |
| **Events & Reunions** | Event creation, invitations, RSVP tracking, and feedback collection |
| **Notifications** | Updates and alerts to keep alumni informed |
| **Reports & Analytics** | Dashboard metrics on registrations, engagement, donations, and events |
